## Product and technical overview

Pebbles preserves recommendations that would otherwise disappear into conversations and group chats. The project focuses on keeping the context behind a recommendation: who shared it and why it was worth saving.

The existing implementation uses TypeScript, NestJS, and MongoDB. The project is in development; this page does not claim a launched product or production outcomes.

## Design priorities

Sharing should be intentional, collections should belong to the people creating them, and recommendations should remain useful without likes, follower counts, or engagement metrics.
