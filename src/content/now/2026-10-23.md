## Building

With the stable releases of Crona out in September, I've moved from shipping features to refining how it feels to use. Most recently that meant [reworking the TUI layout](/notes/2026-10-18-221554) so it reads like a terminal app rather than a web page squeezed into one.

I've also picked **Pebbles** back up after a break.

The portfolio is done, so this site has gone back to being what I wanted it to be: a place to post notes and writing regularly instead of a project in itself.

## Learning

Still going deeper on Go, Linux, and lower-level software, along with SwiftUI through Crona's macOS app.

On the infrastructure side, I'm spending more time on distributed systems and observability.
