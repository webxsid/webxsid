This page changes whenever my priorities do.

## Building

Most of my free time is currently split between **Crona** and this portfolio.

Crona is approaching a point where the foundations feel stable enough to spend more time polishing the experience than redesigning it. The focus lately has been interface clarity, release engineering, packaging, and making the application feel calm to use over long periods.

I'm also slowly bringing this website together. Rather than treating it as a résumé, I'm using it as a place to document projects, decisions, and the thinking behind them.

## Learning

I've been spending more time understanding systems that sit a little closer to the operating system than the application layer.

That currently means Go, SQLite, release tooling, Linux, observability, and becoming more comfortable reading and reasoning about lower-level code instead of always working through abstractions.

Native Apple development has also become a growing interest, particularly SwiftUI and the surrounding ecosystem.

## At work

I'm continuing to build and maintain the backend systems behind Taptic's interview platform.

Most of that work revolves around distributed backend services, automation, infrastructure, and gradually modernising parts of the platform while keeping the production system stable.

## Away from the keyboard

I've been trying to stay consistent with lifting, cycling, and generally spending less time sitting in front of a screen after work.
I'm also reading more technical books than I have in a while and making a conscious effort to write down ideas instead of leaving them in my notes app forever.
