An opinionated capture layer for short-lived notes, ideas, and operational fragments.

Pebbles sits close to the same design space as [Building projects for focus](/writing/blogs/building-projects-for-focus) and [Keeping notes small](/writing/notes/keeping-notes-small): if the user is expected to keep feeding the system, the system needs to feel immediate and readable.

The project benefits from a simple surface because the underlying input problem is already complex enough. A feed of fragments only stays useful if the feed itself does not turn into another source of noise.

That is why I think of the app as a capture layer first and a browsing layer second. The capture step has to be quick; the browse step has to be forgiving.

Related references include [The Cathedral and the Bazaar](/references/the-cathedral-and-the-bazaar) and [Noise and the Shape of Music](/references/noise-and-the-shape-of-music), which are both useful when thinking about systems that have to feel alive without becoming chaotic.
