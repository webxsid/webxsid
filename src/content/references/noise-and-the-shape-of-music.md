## What stayed with me

Some work does not persuade through argument. It persuades through texture.

That changed how I think about presentation, pacing, and the emotional weight of restraint.

## Why it matters

It is a useful reminder that clarity is not always the same thing as simplicity, and that a piece can stay with you because of how it feels, not because of what it says directly.

That is relevant to the site as well. [Building projects for focus](/writing/blogs/building-projects-for-focus) argues for restraint in structure, while this reference argues for restraint in texture. The two are not the same, and it helps to keep them separate.

The same idea shows up in [Rails for quick thoughts](/writing/notes/rails-for-quick-thoughts), where the job of the page is not to overwhelm but to let the right piece of information arrive quickly.
