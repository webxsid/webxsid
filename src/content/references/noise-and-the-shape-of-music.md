## What stayed with me

Some work does not persuade through argument. It persuades through texture.

That changed how I think about presentation, pacing, and the emotional weight of restraint.

## Why it matters

It is a useful reminder that clarity is not always the same thing as simplicity, and that a piece can stay with you because of how it feels, not because of what it says directly.
