## What stayed with me

The point was never just open source. It was that coordination shapes the product as much as the code itself.

That idea has stuck with me every time I’ve had to choose between a controlled system and one that can evolve in the open.

## Ideas I still return to

The useful question is not whether a system is elegant in isolation. It is whether people can participate in it without losing their way.

## Why it matters

It remains one of the clearest reminders that software is social before it is technical.
